# Product goals

**Status:** Phase 1 scaffold. This document will be drafted from the owner-
reviewed decisions in `../plans/phase-1-decisions-brief.md`.

This specification will define:

- the trader and workflow served by the scanner;
- eligible securities and session;
- qualification, ranking, and top-20 behavior;
- aggregate-, trade-, and quote-derived display goals;
- freshness and availability visible to the user;
- the independently deployable UI contract;
- required version 1 capabilities; and
- explicit product non-goals.

Implementation details and provider-specific field mappings do not belong in
this document.
